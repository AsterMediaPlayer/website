Features

What Aster does, on every screen

At release · Apple, Android, Windows and Linux

In short: Aster is the same app on Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android TV, Android phones and tablets, Windows and Linux. Almost everything works everywhere. A few extras belong to one kind of device, like Top Shelf on Apple TV or casting from Android.

See the channels guide to make your own.

On Plex? Use the Channel Builder. A channel's settings live in the Plex playlist's description. The builder at channels.astermediaplayer.com is a form that writes them for you: pick a number, what it plays (genres, networks, collections, years, ratings), how it's scheduled, and the corner logo, then copy one line into the playlist's description.

Sign in with Plex there, and it suggests names from your own library, shows what's in each playlist, and can save the settings, change what's in a playlist, or create a new channel for you, always after asking. Your sign-in stays in your browser and goes only to Plex.

Companion plugins

Free, optional plugins for your Jellyfin server add more to Aster. Install them from Aster's plugin repository, as the support page explains.

  • Aster Disc: DVD and Blu-ray style disc menus, in Aster and in Jellyfin's web app. Guide
  • Aster OP-ED: every anime opening and ending, with song details. Guide
  • Aster Channels: groups for Live TV channels, and a place to manage Aster's Channels in the Jellyfin dashboard. Guide
  • Aster Aurora: an Apple TV-style look for Jellyfin's web app, with a hero banner, trailer and opening backgrounds, and an episodes row.
